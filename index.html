About Me

A technical and creative approach to modern audio production.

I'm Rweye Beatz, a Sound Engineer, Mixing Engineer, Audio Mastering Specialist and Music Producer based in Tanzania. Since 2021, I have worked with artists through the complete audio-production process — from recording and vocal production to mixing and final mastering.


My background in Computer Engineering strengthens my technical approach to audio, while my production experience helps me understand both the creative and technical sides of a record.

Services

Professional audio services for artists, creators and music projects.

🎙️ Recording & Vocal Engineering

Vocal recording, session management, editing, comping, tuning and vocal production.

🎚️ Mixing

Clear, balanced and powerful mixes using EQ, compression, saturation, effects and automation.

🔊 Audio Mastering

Final tonal balance, dynamics, loudness and stereo presentation for digital release.

🎹 Music Production

Beat production, arrangement and creative development across Afrobeat, Afrogospel, R&B and Afro-swing.
